Therapy Appointment Reminder Text Messages
Optional Enrollment

This is optional. You do not have to sign this form. Signing or declining has no effect on your therapy, your appointments, or any care you receive at Perkins Country Manor. Consent to receive text messages is not required to schedule an appointment, to be admitted, or to receive services.

What you will receive if you enroll

  • A text message reminder of the date and time of each scheduled outpatient therapy appointment. You may be asked to reply YES to confirm or NO to cancel.
  • A message if an appointment is rescheduled or cancelled.

You will not receive marketing or promotional messages. These messages are sent by The Engine Room Financial Group on behalf of Perkins Country Manor.

Program disclosures

Program: Therapy Appointment Reminder Service

Message frequency: Varies by your appointment schedule — typically one reminder per scheduled appointment, plus a message if it is rescheduled or cancelled.

Message and data rates may apply. The program itself is free.

To stop: Reply STOP to any message. For help: Reply HELP or call (606) 756-2156.
